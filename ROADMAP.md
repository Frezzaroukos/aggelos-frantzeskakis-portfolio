# Aggelos Frantzeskakis Portfolio — Evolution Roadmap

This document outlines structured phases for evolving the portfolio locally and with AI assistance.

## Phase 1: Content Enrichment & Case Studies
- **Project Detail Views:** Expand modal dialogs into full dedicated subpages or rich multi-section case studies for AXON OSS, Anabasis, Thermidor, and Anafora.
- **Visual Artifacts:** Add UI mockups, architecture diagrams, or workflow screenshots for each repository.
- **Personal Bio & Philosophy:** Write a more expressive narrative on the Profile section detailing your approach to offline-first engineering and local AI.

## Phase 2: Technical & Interactive Upgrades
- **Form Backend:** Connect the contact form to a production serverless endpoint (e.g. Formspree, Resend, or Web3Forms) so messages arrive directly in your inbox.
- **Dynamic GitHub Sync:** Integrate GitHub GraphQL or REST queries with client-side caching to automatically reflect live star counts, recent commits, and new public repositories.
- **Interactive Demos:** Embed lightweight interactive widgets or browser playable embeds for public PWAs and utilities.

## Phase 3: Performance, Polish & Deployment
- **Design System Extensions:** Refine typography scales, subtle micro-interactions, and high-contrast accessibility compliance.
- **Analytics & SEO:** Add privacy-focused analytics (such as Umami) and OpenGraph meta tags for rich social sharing previews.
- **Production Publishing:** Keep the GitHub repository private during local AI iterations; publish and bind a custom domain when the portfolio reaches its final professional state.
